# Nimbus Labs Pvt. Ltd. — Expense and Reimbursement Policy

> Synthetic sample document written for testing Courtroom RAG. Nimbus Labs is a made-up company.

Policy owner: Finance
Version: 2.4 (effective 1 June 2026)

## 1. General principles

Employees will be reimbursed for reasonable expenses that are necessary for doing their job and that are approved in advance where this policy requires. All claims must be filed on the expense portal within 45 days of the expense, along with an itemised bill or GST invoice. Claims filed after 45 days will not be reimbursed unless the CFO approves the exception.

## 2. Hardware and laptops

Laptops are provided by the IT team and remain company property. Employees must not buy their own laptop and claim the cost as an expense. Personal laptop purchases are not reimbursable under any category, including for interns.

Small accessories such as a mouse, keyboard, headset or laptop stand can be claimed up to ₹4,000 per year without prior approval. Monitors are covered under the home setup allowance in the Remote Work Policy, not under this policy.

## 3. Learning and development

Each full-time employee has a learning budget of ₹25,000 per financial year. It can be used for online courses, certifications, books and conference tickets that relate to the employee's current role or a role they are preparing for internally.

Learning expenses above ₹10,000 need manager pre-approval. If an employee resigns within six months of a reimbursed certification costing more than ₹15,000, the company may recover the cost from the final settlement.

Interns have a learning budget of ₹5,000 for the duration of the internship, limited to online courses and books.

## 4. Travel

Domestic travel for work must be booked through the company travel desk. Economy class is the default for flights. Employees at Director level and above may book premium economy for flights longer than three hours.

Daily allowance for food during work travel is ₹1,500 per day in metro cities and ₹1,000 per day in other cities. Alcohol is not reimbursable.

Local cab rides are reimbursable when travelling to client sites or when working past 21:00 in the office. Daily commute between home and office is not reimbursable.

## 5. Team meals and celebrations

Managers can spend up to ₹1,000 per team member per quarter on team lunches or celebrations. This budget does not roll over to the next quarter.

## 6. Mobile and internet

Employees in Sales and Customer Success receive a mobile bill reimbursement of up to ₹800 per month. Other employees are not eligible unless they are on an approved on-call rotation, in which case they receive ₹500 per month while on rotation.

## 7. Non-reimbursable items

The following are never reimbursed: personal entertainment, traffic fines, personal grooming, gym memberships (covered separately by the wellness benefit), and any expense without a valid bill.
