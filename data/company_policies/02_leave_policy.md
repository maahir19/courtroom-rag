## 1. Types of leave

Full-time employees are entitled to the following leave in each calendar year:

- Earned leave (EL): 18 days, credited at 1.5 days per month.
- Sick leave (SL): 10 days, credited at the start of the year.
- Casual leave (CL): 6 days, credited at the start of the year.
- Public holidays: 10 fixed holidays and 2 optional (restricted) holidays from the published list.

Employees who join mid-year receive leave on a pro-rata basis.

## 2. Carry forward

Up to 30 days of unused earned leave can be carried forward into the next year. Earned leave above 30 days at the end of the year lapses on 31 December.

Sick leave and casual leave cannot be carried forward. Any unused sick or casual leave lapses at the end of the calendar year.

## 3. Leave encashment

Leave encashment is not offered at year end. Unused earned leave is not paid out every year; it is only carried forward subject to the 30-day limit in Section 2.

Earned leave is encashed only at the time of separation (resignation or termination), up to a maximum of 30 days, calculated on basic salary. Sick leave and casual leave are never encashed.

As a one-time exception for the 2025 transition to the new HR system, employees who had more than 30 days of earned leave on 31 December 2025 were allowed to encash up to 10 excess days. This exception does not apply to any later year.

## 4. Applying for leave

Planned leave of more than three consecutive working days must be applied for at least two weeks in advance. Managers should respond within three working days. Leave is not considered approved until it shows as approved on the HR portal.

Sick leave of more than two consecutive days needs a medical certificate uploaded within a week of returning to work.

## 5. Sabbatical

Employees with at least four years of continuous service can request an unpaid sabbatical of one to three months. Approval depends on business needs and is decided by the department head. During a sabbatical, health insurance continues but leave does not accrue.

## 6. Parental leave

Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of paid maternity leave as per law. Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave, to be taken within six months of the child's birth or adoption.

## 7. Interns

Interns receive 1 day of leave per month of internship and are not eligible for leave carry forward or encashment.
