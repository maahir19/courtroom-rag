# Nimbus Labs Pvt. Ltd. — Remote Work Policy

> Synthetic sample document written for testing Courtroom RAG. Nimbus Labs is a made-up company.

Policy owner: People Operations
Version: 3.2 (effective 1 April 2026)
Applies to: All full-time employees in India. Interns and contract staff are covered only where stated.

## 1. Purpose

Nimbus Labs supports flexible work because it helps people do focused work and reduces commute time. At the same time, product teams need regular in-person collaboration. This policy explains who can work remotely, for how long, and what approvals are needed.

## 2. Hybrid schedule

Full-time employees follow a hybrid model. Employees are expected to be in the office at least two days per week, with Tuesday as the common team day. The remaining days can be worked from home (WFH) after informing the reporting manager on the team calendar.

Teams may set a stricter in-office requirement during release weeks, onboarding of new joiners, or quarterly planning. Managers must announce such weeks at least five working days in advance.

## 3. Fully remote arrangements

A fully remote arrangement is allowed only for roles marked "Remote eligible" in the job catalogue. Engineering roles in Platform and Data teams are generally remote eligible. Customer Success, Hardware Lab and Facilities roles are not remote eligible.

Employees who want a fully remote arrangement must have completed at least 12 months with the company and must have a performance rating of "Meets expectations" or higher in their most recent review cycle. Approval is needed from both the reporting manager and the department head.

## 4. Working from another city in India

Employees may work from another city within India for up to 30 calendar days in a year without changing their base location. This counts as WFH and the hybrid office-day requirement is relaxed during this period, provided the manager approves in advance on the HR portal.

Stays longer than 30 days require a formal location change request, which may affect the employee's salary band, because pay bands are linked to the base city.

## 5. Working from outside India

Working from outside India is not permitted as a general rule. This is because of tax, payroll, data protection and immigration obligations that Nimbus Labs cannot manage for individual employees.

A temporary exception of up to 14 calendar days per year may be granted for personal reasons (for example, a family emergency or extending a holiday). The exception requires written approval from the department head, the Legal team and the Information Security team. Employees must use a company laptop with the VPN always on, and must not access the production customer database while abroad.

Requests to work from outside India for longer than 14 days will be rejected. Working from abroad without approval is treated as a policy violation and may lead to disciplinary action.

## 6. Interns and contract staff

Interns are expected to work from the office at least four days per week, since mentoring is the main purpose of the internship. Interns may work from home on other days with mentor approval. Interns are not eligible for fully remote arrangements or for working from outside India.

Contract staff follow the terms written in their contract with the vendor agency.

## 7. Equipment and internet allowance

Employees on hybrid or remote arrangements receive a one-time home setup allowance of ₹15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor. Fully remote employees additionally receive an internet allowance of ₹1,200 per month. Interns are not eligible for the home setup allowance.

## 8. Review of arrangements

Remote arrangements are reviewed every six months. The company may withdraw a fully remote arrangement if there are repeated availability issues, missed core hours (11:00 to 16:00 IST), or a drop in performance rating. Employees will get 30 days' notice before being asked to return to the hybrid schedule.
