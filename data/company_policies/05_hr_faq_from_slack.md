# People Ops FAQ (compiled from #ask-peopleops Slack channel)

> Synthetic sample document written for testing Courtroom RAG. Nimbus Labs is a made-up company. Answers here are informal; if they conflict with a policy document, the policy document wins.

## Can I work from Goa for a month?

Yes, as long as your manager approves it on the HR portal before you go. Working from another city in India for up to 30 days a year is allowed. Remember Tuesday team days are relaxed during this period, but you still need to be online during core hours.

## My parents live in Dubai. Can I work from there for three weeks during Diwali?

Unfortunately no — the maximum exception for working outside India is 14 days, and it needs approval from your department head, Legal and InfoSec. You could take part of the trip as earned leave and work for up to 14 days with approval.

## Do unused leaves get paid out in December?

No. People keep asking this because of the one-time encashment we did in December 2025 when we moved HR systems. That was a one-off. Unused earned leave is carried forward (up to 30 days); anything above that lapses.

## I'm an intern. Can I get the ₹15,000 home setup allowance?

No, the home setup allowance is for full-time employees only. Interns can claim small accessories like a mouse or headset under the ₹4,000 accessory limit in the expense policy, if their mentor agrees.

## Can I buy a MacBook myself and get reimbursed? IT is taking too long.

No. Laptops must come from IT and personal laptop purchases are not reimbursable. If you are blocked, raise a P1 ticket with IT and tag your manager — loaner laptops are usually available within two working days.

## Can I use ChatGPT-style tools for work?

Yes, if it is one of the company-approved AI tools and you are not pasting customer personal data, credentials or confidential financial info. Check the approved list on the InfoSec wiki.

## Is gym membership covered?

Not as an expense claim. There's a separate wellness benefit of ₹6,000 per year that covers gyms, yoga classes and sports clubs. You claim it through the benefits portal, not the expense portal.
