# Nimbus Labs Pvt. Ltd. — Information Security Policy (Employee Summary)

> Synthetic sample document written for testing Courtroom RAG. Nimbus Labs is a made-up company.

Policy owner: Information Security
Version: 4.1 (effective 15 February 2026)

## 1. Devices

Company data may only be accessed from company-managed devices that have disk encryption, endpoint protection and automatic updates enabled. Personal phones may be used for email and chat only after installing the mobile device management (MDM) profile.

Personal laptops must never be used to access source code, the production environment, or customer data.

## 2. Network

The VPN must be switched on whenever an employee works outside the office network. Public Wi-Fi at cafes, airports or hotels is allowed only with the VPN on. Employees must not share hotspot passwords or leave laptops unlocked in public places.

## 3. Access to customer data

Access to the production customer database is granted on a need-to-know basis and reviewed every quarter. Access from outside India is blocked by default because of data residency commitments in our customer contracts. Temporary access from abroad can only be enabled by the Information Security team and is logged.

## 4. Use of AI tools

Employees may use company-approved AI assistants for drafting, coding and summarising, as long as no customer personal data, credentials or unreleased financial information is pasted into them. Public AI tools that are not on the approved list must not be used with any internal document marked "Confidential" or above.

## 5. Passwords and authentication

All accounts must use the company password manager and multi-factor authentication. Sharing passwords, even with teammates, is not permitted. Shared service accounts must be stored in the team vault.

## 6. Reporting incidents

Lost or stolen devices, phishing emails and suspected data leaks must be reported to security@nimbuslabs.example within one hour of noticing them. Employees will not be penalised for reporting an honest mistake quickly; delays in reporting are treated more seriously than the mistake itself.
