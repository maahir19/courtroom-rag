# What I found out about Quillstack (research notes)

> Synthetic sample notes written for testing Courtroom RAG. All companies and people are fictional.

## Funding and finances

- Series A: $12M in March 2025, led by a well-known developer-tools fund.
- A senior I know who works there (Arjun, joined 2024) said the company has "around two years of runway" according to the last all-hands, which would mean money until roughly early 2027 without new funding.
- Arjun said revenue grew about 3x last year and they recently signed two large enterprise customers. Leadership is "talking to investors" for a Series B.

## Layoffs and stability

- In October 2025 Quillstack laid off 11 people (about 8% of the company at that time), mostly from the sales team after a failed expansion into the US mid-market. No engineers were affected, according to Arjun.
- Since then they have been hiring engineers again, which is how I got this offer.

## Culture and work hours

- Arjun said engineering culture is strong: code reviews are serious, there are design docs for every big change, and junior engineers get to present at the weekly tech talk.
- He also said that during release weeks people sometimes work late, and on-call can be "rough" — the on-call rotation is weekly, about once every six weeks for each engineer.
- Two other people I talked to on LinkedIn said they liked the learning but felt the pace was intense. One left after 18 months because of burnout.

## Growth

- Engineers who joined at the junior level in 2023 are now leading small projects.
- The engineering ladder has four levels below Staff, and there is a published promotion rubric.

## Unknowns

- I don't know the current valuation, so I can't really estimate what my ESOPs might be worth.
- I don't know if they'd negotiate on the parental insurance or increase the base.
